## Guidelines for Attending Help Sessions
- Do not come to a help session without first attempting the assignment on your own.
- Arrive prepared and organized, knowing what you need to ask. Be ready to share any work you have already completed on the assignment.
- Remember that help session staff are there to help you find answers on your own, not to provide answers for you.
- Be professional, polite, and courteous at all times. If you do not understand a particular explanation, ask politely to have it repeated.
