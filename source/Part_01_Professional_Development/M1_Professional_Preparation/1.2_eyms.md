```{rubric} Engineering Your Major Events
```
Engineering Your Major (EYM) events provide direct insight into specific engineering majors. These sessions cover career pathways, degree plans, specialization areas, and firsthand student experiences to help you make an informed choice.

You must attend **four (4) events** total this semester, including one (1) from **each group** below and one additional event from any group of your choice.

  **Group A:** 
  - Agricultural Engineering
  - Biological Engineering 
  - Environmental and Natural Resources Engineering
  - Construction Engineering and Management
  - Sustainability and Environmental Engineering
  - Nuclear Engineering

  **Group B:** 
  - Civil Engineering
  - Industrial Engineering
  - Materials Engineering
  - Multidisciplinary Engineering
  - Chemical Engineering

  **Group C:**
  - Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering
  - Biomedical Engineering
  - Electrical Engineering
  - Computer Engineering
  - Mechqanical Engineering

For complete details on requirements, review the **FYE-EYMs-Engineering Your Major** course on Brightspace.

```{admonition} Are you already accepted into your engineering major? 
   :class: warning
If you have already transitioned or been accepted directly into your engineering major, please check in with your instructor or GTA to discuss your specific EYM requirements.
```
