# Professional Preparation

This module introduces students to the core professional skills required in both the ENGR 131 classroom and future engineering careers. Because engineering problems are complex and impact society, engineers must collaborate effectively, communicate clearly, and make ethical decisions based on reliable information.

Beyond immediate course expectations, this module focuses on building transferable skills for professional environments.

- Reflecting on and improving problem-solving processes
- Professional communication through written, visual, and oral methods
