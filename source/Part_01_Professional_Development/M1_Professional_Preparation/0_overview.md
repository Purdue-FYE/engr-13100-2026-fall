# Professional Preparation & Career Exploration

This module and its assignments introduce you to the core professional skills and career pathways required for success in ENGR 131 and your future as an engineer. Because engineering challenges are complex and deeply impact society, modern engineers must collaborate effectively, communicate clearly, and make ethical, data-driven decisions. Beyond immediate course expectations, this foundational work provides a structured space for career exploration—allowing you to dive deeply into the diverse disciplines within the College of Engineering, discover areas of specialization, and strategically align your personal passions with an academic plan of study.

```{rubric} Topics Covered
```
- Reflecting on and improving problem-solving processes
- Professional communication through written, visual, and oral methods
- Adhering to course policies, including academic integrity policies
- Exploring engineering majors, plans of study, industry sectors, and career opportunities
