ENGR 131 covers the following topics during the semester:

- Data analytics (calculations, descriptive statistics, histograms, probability,
  charts, and regression).

- Academic integrity and ethics.

- Communication (reports, peer review, and information literacy).

- Teaming (team dynamics, team member roles, diversity, and code of cooperation).

- Team projects (modeling and design).

- {program}`Excel` basics (calculations, importing data, relational operators,
  plotting data).

- {program}`Python` basics (scripts, data types, importing data, relational
  operators, logical operators, plotting data, user-defined functions, selection
  structures, while loops, for loops, and nested loops).

- Overview of engineering majors offered by Purdue University.
