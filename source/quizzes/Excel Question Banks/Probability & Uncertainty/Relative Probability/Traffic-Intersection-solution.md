You are a civil engineer collecting data at the traffic intersection by Armstrong Hall. Every 30 seconds, you record the state of a traffic light:
- 1: Green
- 2: Yellow
- 3: Red
- 4: Turn arrow

At the end of 10 minutes, you counted the frequency of each state:
| State | Frequency |
|:---- | ----: |
| 1 | 9 |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 4 |


## a) Using relative probability, what proportion of the measurements was the light not in state 4?

### solution: 9 + 3 + 4 / 20 = 16/20 = 4/5

- [ ] Correct/incorrect

## b) Using relative probability, what proportion of the measurements was the light yellow?

### solution: 3/20
Sample rubric:
- [ ] Correct/incorrect

## c) Your supervisor asks you how many hours in a 24-hour period the light is yellow. In one to two sentences, briefly explain how you would answer?

### sample solution:
$$
\frac{3}{20} = \frac{x}{24}
$$

$$
x = \frac{24 \cdot 3}{20}
  = \frac{6 \cdot 3}{5}
  = \frac{18}{5}
  = 3.6 \text{ hours}
$$

Given this data, we could extrapolate that the light is yellow 3.6 hours in a 24 hour period, but this may not be accurate because our measurements only came from a 10-minute period with infrequent measurements. I would collect more data throughout the day for a more accurate conclusion.
